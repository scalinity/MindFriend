## Compatibility and recovery

- Missing session support never becomes a fake synchronized state. Missing new privacy support fails closed; it cannot route personal context through an old unsafe path. Local activities, drafts, history/correction and Help remain available without paid inference.
- Operation IDs and transmitted payloads are immutable. Preserve uncertain operations and reconcile the same identity. Do not replay paid work under a new ID after timeout. Memory Generate dispatch couples quota and its durable compare-and-set; a lost committed dispatch can consume one admission without a provider call, but cannot debit or generate twice.
- Existing generic same-owner `award_xp` remains available for retained callers. The new forward guard denies anonymous/cross-owner requests. Exactly-once claims apply to the tested atomic phone/offline/companion mood and quest routes, support-grounding and session-keyed exercise reward routes; old clients issuing a separate generic reward call remain a release-compatibility concern.
- Stop using, Forget, Delete conversation and Delete session have distinct scopes. Suppressions prevent identified sources and their known derived closure from entering new context; they do not erase content already transmitted to a provider or promise semantic recognition of every paraphrase.
- Export includes the fixed listed cloud datasets and the protected local support journal, with omissions and unsent/rejected transcript state explicit. Private Vault remains device-only and outside the export/context change. No export is advertised as everything ever held.
- New schema is additive, while privacy/reward guards alter supported write behavior. Before any real rollout, retain a database backup and the exact pending plan, qualify the previous compatible client/backend combination, and prefer a reviewed forward repair. Do not roll back by deleting journals, dropping support tables or replaying accepted input.
