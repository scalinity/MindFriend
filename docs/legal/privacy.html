2. Information We Collect

We collect the kinds of information below. Some of it may count as sensitive under certain laws (e.g., "special category" data under GDPR or "sensitive personal information" under CPRA).

2.1 Account and profile information

  • Identifiers: user ID, username/handle, display name
  • Sign-in details: your Sign in with Apple identifier, Google sign-in identifier, or email address and password, depending on how you sign in
  • Sign-up details: your birth year, your confirmation that you are 13 or older, and the date and version of the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy you accepted
  • Profile details: the avatar photo you upload or create, timezone, language, notification preferences, privacy settings (including Privacy Mode) and your AI tone preference
  • Contact details you type for an invite: the email address or phone number of a friend, family member or therapist you invite
  • Therapist or coach application: if you apply, the details you enter, such as the name to show, license number and state, specialties, languages and rates

2.2 Wellness and mental health-related information (sensitive)

You may give us information about your mental health and wellbeing, including:

  • Mood check-ins: mood score, anxiety and energy ratings, times, and any notes
  • Journal entries, reflections and thought records
  • Voice journals: the audio recordings you make and their transcripts
  • AI conversations: the messages you send, the AI's replies, and for live voice conversations, the confirmed text of each turn. See §6.7 for what each AI feature sends and to which provider.
  • Memory: things saved so the AI can remember you, such as saved memories, memory suggestions waiting for your review, and today's focus (see §6.8)
  • Safety Plan: the plan you write in the app
  • Decisions and values: decisions you think through in the app and the personal values you rank
  • Wellness activity history: quests assigned, completed or skipped, exercise sessions, streaks and badges
  • Safety records: when the app shows you a safety response, we record that it was shown, the time, and the safety keyword that triggered it. We do not copy your message into this record.

Important: You choose what you share. Please avoid entering information you do not want processed (e.g., highly sensitive identifiers).

2.3 Social and community information

If you use community features (Circles, mentorship, peer support, partner and family):

2.8 Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not intentionally ask for:

2.9 Optional Apple Health data

Apple Health sync. If you connect Apple Health (HealthKit) and turn on sync, MindFriend reads the health categories you allow and uploads health summaries and workout records to your account in Supabase. These may include sleep duration and timing, heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV), steps, distance, active energy, exercise and mindful minutes, and workout details such as type, start/end times and duration.

Sleep tracking. Separately from that sync setting, if you allow MindFriend to read sleep data, opening the Sleep screen saves your nightly sleep details from Apple Health to your account: sleep stages, heart rate, HRV and breathing rate during sleep, for recent nights and earlier nights still in Apple Health.

These records support your sleep and health history, personal baselines, correlations and insights. They are stored with our cloud provider and handled under this Policy's retention, security and data-rights sections. Health data is never sent to an AI provider.

To stop it: turn off MindFriend's access in the Health app (or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health). Choosing "Disconnect Apple Health" in MindFriend stops the sync, but it does not stop sleep tracking and does not delete data already uploaded. Deleting your account removes the uploaded data, or you can email us to ask for it to be removed.

2.10 Optional calendar access

If you allow calendar access, the daily briefing uses the titles, times and locations of your upcoming events today (up to five). They are stored with that briefing in your account. No AI is used for this, and calendar events are never sent to an AI provider.

2.11 Private Vault (stays on your device)

Private Vault entries are encrypted and stay only on your device. They are never uploaded to us, never sent to an AI provider, not included in iCloud or device backups, and not part of Export Data. Because we never have them, we cannot recover them. Private Vault is separate from Memory, which is kept in your account and can be used as chat context (see §6.8).

6.8 Memory, Privacy Mode and your choices

Memory is kept in your account so the AI can remember you: saved memories (things you add, or suggestions you approve), suggestions waiting for your review, and today's focus. You can see and change it on the "What MindFriend remembers" screen. Memory is different from Private Vault, which stays only on your device (see §2.11).

  • Privacy Mode (You → Settings and account → Privacy): Standard is the default. Enhanced keeps your saved memories, today's focus and other personal context (such as time since your last visit) out of chat requests, and turns off automatic memory extraction and memory suggestions. Your message, recent conversation history and AI tone preference are still sent so the AI can reply.
  • Stop using keeps a memory but leaves it out of AI requests. You can choose Use again later.
  • Forget deletes a memory from our servers and stops it being used again. It does not delete the conversation the memory came from.
  • Dismiss turns down a suggestion so it is never saved.
  • Neither Stop using nor Forget can take back copies already sent to an AI provider.
  • Safety Plan: the Safety Plan screen has an "Allow AI to reference my Safety Plan" switch. No AI feature reads your Safety Plan at present; if one does in future, it will follow that switch.
  • Not using a feature is always a choice: nothing is sent for an AI feature you do not use.

6.9 Context records

For AI chat and voice replies, we keep a record of which kinds of saved context were used (by reference, not the text itself), which provider and model replied, and what was left out. You can see it under Context in the chat. These records are included in Export Data and are deleted when you delete the conversation or your account.

8. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption in transit: TLS/HTTPS for data sent between your device and our servers
  • Encryption at rest: stored data is encrypted by our database provider
  • On-device encryption: Private Vault entries are encrypted on your device and never leave it
  • Access controls: role-based access, least privilege, strong authentication for internal tools
  • Row Level Security: database rules so users can only reach their own data
  • Crash report scrubbing: crash reports are linked to your account ID only, never your name or email, and replays hide all text and images
  • Rate limiting: protection against abuse of accounts and AI features
  • Secure development practices: code review, dependency updates, and testing

No system can be guaranteed 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your device and account sign-in details secure.

12. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

MindFriend is a mobile app. It does not use cookies, has no analytics or advertising SDK, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Crash reporting (Sentry). The app sends Sentry crash and error reports, performance measurements from some sessions, and a log of recent app actions (screen names, feature events and network request timings). For some sessions it also sends a masked replay of the screen layout and taps, with all text and images hidden. These reports are linked to your account ID, never your name or email. There is no in-app setting to turn crash reporting off.

Website analytics

The website at getmindfriend.app measures how it is used with Umami, open-source analytics software that we run on our own server (hosted by Hostinger in the United States); no analytics company receives this data. It does not use cookies, does not store anything on your device, and does not follow you across other websites. For each visit it records the pages you view, the referring site and campaign (UTM) tags, browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language, approximate location (country, region and city, looked up from your IP address), page-load performance measurements, and which links and buttons you select, such as the App Store link. Visits are grouped under a pseudonymous identifier calculated as a one-way hash of the website, your IP address, your browser’s user agent and a value that changes every month, so visits cannot be linked from one month to the next. Your IP address is used for that calculation and the location lookup and is not stored.

The website also records where on a page you click and how far you scroll, and a session replay: a recording of the page’s layout, scrolling, mouse movement and clicks that shows how the page behaved during your visit. Anything typed into a form field is masked in the browser before it is sent, so it is never recorded. We use this only to find layout and usability problems on the website. Website analytics are kept for 13 months and session replays for 30 days (unless we save a specific recording to investigate a website problem); both are deleted automatically by a nightly cleanup.

Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): the app does not ask to track you, because it does not use the advertising identifier
  • Notifications: you can turn off push notifications in iOS Settings
  • Crash reporting: there is no in-app setting to turn it off
